# Disclaimer

**Not investment advice.** TradeArena X is a strategy research and education
tool. It operates on synthetic, randomly generated price data and has no
connection to any market data feed, exchange, or broker. It cannot place an
order or move capital, and nothing it outputs is a recommendation to trade any
instrument.

The agent performance figures it reports — Sharpe ratios, drawdowns, inventory
risk — are measured against a simulated order book. They describe how a
strategy behaved against this simulator and say nothing about how it would
behave in a real market, where fill probability, latency, adverse selection and
market impact all apply and none of them are modelled here.
